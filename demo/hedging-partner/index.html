Hedging-Partner Recommender

A stock recommender that keeps the investor's taste but, among the stocks they would plausibly buy, prefers the ones that lower the risk of the portfolio they already hold. Each example is a real purchase from the FAR-Trans dataset: the customer's actual holdings on the day before, the collaborative-filtering (CF) favourite, and the hedging partner our risk gate picks from the same top list. Class project for Deep Learning Recommender Systems (HUFS, fall 2026).

Setup
  • Data. FAR-Trans (CC BY 4.0): buys and sells of a European bank's retail customers, 2018–2022, with daily closes. Holdings are rebuilt from the transactions, valued at the last close before the purchase day, and include funds and bonds. The closes are not adjusted for corporate actions, so a daily move beyond the Athens Exchange's ±30% limit (e.g. Attica Bank's 2021 rights issues) counts as no change, and price histories are back-adjusted the same way.
  • Taste. WMF trained on purchases before 2022-02-09; the 94 Athens-listed stocks the customer does not hold are ranked by its score.
  • Risk. Ledoit–Wolf covariance of the 250 daily log returns before the purchase day. Adding a stock at weight a changes the portfolio variance to (1−a)²σ²P + 2a(1−a)σP,i + a²σ²i; the default weight is 1/(n+1) for n holdings.
  • Gate. The top-5 list may only change for clear gains: a stock ranked 6–10 by taste can enter if it cuts predicted volatility by at least 2%, replacing one that does not. The hedging partner is the list's most risk-reducing stock other than the CF favourite.
  • Check. “기간별 리스크 변화” is realized, not predicted: the volatility over the following trading days with the stock added at the same weight, against holding nothing new.
  • Examples. Selected, not representative: test purchases (May–Sep 2022) by customers with 3–8 priced holdings where the CF favourite would raise predicted risk and the partner cuts it by 5%+, the three largest gaps per MiFID risk level.

Research artefact; not investment advice. Prices in euro.
