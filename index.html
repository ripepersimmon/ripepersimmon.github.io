About Me

I am an undergraduate researcher in the Financial Engineering Lab. (advised by Prof. Hyeongwoo Kong) at the Division of Finance & AI, Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (HUFS). I work at the intersection of quantitative finance and machine learning. My research interests center on Portfolio Optimization, Decision-Focused Learning, and Large Language Models in Finance, with secondary interests in graph neural networks, synthetic data, and explainable AI.

I plan to pursue an integrated M.S./Ph.D. in Financial Engineering beginning in 2027.
