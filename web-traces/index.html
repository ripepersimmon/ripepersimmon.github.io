What this is (click to fold)

Three open-weight language models (Qwen3-8B, Mistral-Small-24B-Instruct, Gemma4-12B-it) run the same three-turn research loop over a live news search engine (Bing News, past month; results are snapshot-cached, so every arm sees byte-identical results for identical queries). Turn 1: the model writes four search queries about the company. Turn 2: it sees the returned result page (headline + snippet) and chooses four results to open. Turn 3: it reads the articles, writes a memo and gives a buy/sell decision.

Arms differ only in the intervention applied to the model: baseline; a single-neuron additive shift (the “dial”, one MLP neuron, one scalar); a decision-token logit offset calibrated to the same decision index π = (buy − sell)/(buy + sell); contrastive activation addition (CAA); a second neuron from the same model; and write-norm-matched placebo neurons. Direction labels (▲ favourable / ▼ unfavourable / • neutral, for an investor in the company) are given per sentence of headline + snippet by an un-intervened Qwen3-8B judge, or by Gemini. 150 held-out S&P 500 companies × 2 trials (decision-option order counterbalanced), greedy decoding.

Pick a company and two arms to compare what the model asked, what the engine returned, what it opened and what it wrote. Yellow rows are results that appear only on that side; a blue bar marks an opened result. Companion material for “Your AI, On a Dial” (arXiv:2608.22852). Research artefact; not investment advice. ← ripepersimmon.github.io
